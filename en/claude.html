Service capabilities, account requirements and order support conditions.

What do you get

Account requirements

  • Before connecting PRO or MAX, the account must be on the FREE plan.
  • Before placing an order, test the account by sending any message in Claude on the free plan. If Claude accepts the request and responds, there are no apparent restrictions on the account.
  • The recommended account age is at least 7 days. New accounts are more likely to be subject to Anthropic's automated security checks.
  • If the account was registered recently, we recommend waiting about 6 hours and using the free version for a while before connecting a subscription.
  • Follow Claude's rules while using the service. Frequent IP address or region changes, third-party software, mass registration, or sharing the account with other people increase the risk of restrictions.
  • Claude Pro is activated automatically without sharing your login or password. Max 5x and Max 20x require access to your account: after payment, a manager will contact you to request the details needed for activation. You do not enter your login or password in the order form.

How connection works

  1. Select a PRO or MAX plan and place an order.
  2. After payment, the site will show the details required for the selected connection method.
  3. We activate the subscription. Connection usually takes 5–20 minutes, but additional service checks may increase the processing time.
  4. After connection, check access to the selected plan's features. Support will help verify the result if necessary.

  • An account block or restriction imposed by Anthropic is not a warranty case when it is related to the account's condition, violation of service rules, frequent IP address or region changes, third-party software, or actions by the account owner.

  • If the tariff is connected through the App Store or Google Play, the expiration date may not be displayed in the Claude interface. The subscription is valid for the paid period.
