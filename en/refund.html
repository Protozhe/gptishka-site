Refund policy

Refunds depend on the grounds for the claim, paid order terms and applicable law. The new revision applies to orders paid after its publication.

Product delivery and order contents

Product contents, term and price are disclosed before payment. Delivering instructions or initial activation does not end the guarantee for the paid subscription term.

Non-fulfilment or defects

If an order is not fulfilled for reasons on our side, we refund the full amount without fees or expense deductions. Statutory remedies apply to defects, including a full refund where legally justified.

Replacement or reactivation instead of a refund due requires the buyer's agreement.

Voluntary cancellation

For voluntary cancellation before activation, only actual, documented expenses for that order may be deducted, capped at 20% of the payment. This is a maximum, not a fixed fee. Without such expenses, the refund is full.

After a service starts, the calculation accounts for the properly provided portion and legally deductible documented expenses. Reimbursed expenses are excluded. The same amounts are not counted twice. We provide the calculation and evidence.

Early subscription termination

The guarantee covers the entire paid term. If an active subscription ends early, we refund its unused portion unless an exception under the Offer is substantiated.

Refund = price paid for the subscription × unused duration ÷ total paid duration.

The period runs from the confirmed loss of access until the paid term ends. If the exact time is unknown, we use the confirmed date with rounding in the buyer's favour. The amount is rounded to kopecks.

For example, a 30-day subscription priced at RUB 3,000 with 10 unused days gives a RUB 1,000 refund. No additional 20% deduction or fees apply to a guarantee refund. Broader statutory remedies remain available.

Account suspension and platform decisions

We examine the reason for suspension and its connection with the order. Restrictions, checks or a rejected appeal do not by themselves establish the buyer's fault.

When applying a guarantee exception, we identify the applicable rule, established breach by the buyer and its connection with termination. Activation and payment errors on our side or our provider's side are not breaches by the buyer.

Reimbursement by a platform or provider

The amount and timing of reimbursement to us do not automatically determine the buyer's refund. If expenses deducted for voluntary cancellation are later reimbursed to us, we adjust the calculation and refund any excess deduction.

How to submit a claim

Contact support with the order number or information identifying the payment. Describe the issue and include any available access-loss dates and platform notices. Passwords, CVV, login codes and a full card number are not needed.

Timeframes

Initial review takes place within 72 hours. Claims covered by Article 31 of the Consumer Protection Law are satisfied within ten calendar days of submission. Other claims follow the period established by law or contract.

Requests for materials, waiting for a platform and bank processing do not extend the seller's mandatory deadline. Refunds use the original payment method or another agreed and permitted method. We provide the amount, grounds and transaction details.

If a dispute remains unresolved

Contacting support does not limit the right to contact a bank, public authority or court, or to choose a court where provided by law.
